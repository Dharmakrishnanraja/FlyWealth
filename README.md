FLYWEALTH

FLYWEALTH is a smart wellness and business planning web application developed to simplify product presentations, income visualization, and structured team growth analysis for FlyWellness users and business associates.

The app provides an interactive and mobile-friendly experience that helps users understand wellness product concepts, business opportunities, projected earnings, and network growth models in a simple and professional way.

Key Features

- FlyWellness product information and overview
- Interactive income and growth calculator
- Team structure and level-based visualization
- Mobile-optimized responsive design
- QR code and link sharing support
- Fast lightweight HTML-based web app
- Suitable for seminars, business meetings, and presentations

Purpose

FLYWEALTH is designed to make wellness business presentations more modern, visual, and user-friendly while helping users better understand structured growth opportunities.
